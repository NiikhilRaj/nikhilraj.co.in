Plan Sync

A timetable app for college students. It started as a weekend side project and grew into the app thousands of students at KIIT open before their first class. I co-founded it, built its first backend, and later rebuilt both the backend and the UI.

The problem

Where is my next class?

Timetables at a big university change by section, by elective and by week, and they usually live in a PDF or a group chat. Plan Sync puts each student's own schedule on their phone, kept up to date by the community: students add their timetables and report anything that's wrong.

What began as a weekend project for friends had real pull, so I pushed to productize it early and took on the roadmap, feature decisions and outreach to get it in front of the rest of campus.

Backend

From zero to 1,000, then faster

I designed and built the first database and backend on Supabase and GCP, which carried the app to its first 1,000 users.

As usage grew, I redesigned the backend for speed: database indexes on the hot queries, asynchronous processing for work that didn't need to block a request, and response caching for data many students request at once.

API query latency dropped by 76%.

Product & UI

An app that works on campus Wi-Fi

I designed the current cross-platform UI and led three features: electives, so a schedule reflects the courses a student actually picked; search; and network-error handling, so the app degrades gracefully instead of failing on a flaky connection.
